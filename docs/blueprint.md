# **App Name**: Personal Showcase

## Core Features:

- Bio Section: Display a concise professional summary with a brief bio and key skills, and photo in the left side.
- Research Experience: Showcase research experience, highlighting key projects, methodologies, and outcomes.
- Achievements: Dedicated section for achievements, awards, and notable mentions.
- Contact & CV: Display contact information, including email and links to social media profiles, along with a downloadable CV.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: White (#FFFFFF) for a clean and modern feel.
- Secondary color: Light gray (#F0F0F0) for backgrounds and subtle accents.
- Accent: Teal (#008080) for interactive elements and highlights.
- Use a clean, sans-serif font for headings and body text to maintain readability and minimalism.
- Simple, outline-style icons to represent different sections and social media links.
- Grid-based layout with generous white space for organized content presentation.
- Subtle fade-in animations for content sections on scroll.
